1.0 Site Context & Coordinate Reference

The designated project plot for the new Sustainable Innovation Pavilion encompasses Sector B within the eastern academic perimeter. Geodetic and cadastral datasets procured via university land surveys have been established utilizing the WGS 84 geographic reference datum.

The Lead Architectural team must verify that this precise reference frame is adopted within the Common Data Environment (CDE) prior to federating site topography and setting up the conceptual massing project space.

2.0 Volumetric Constraints & Level of Information

To preserve campus sightlines and minimize solar shadow envelopes cast upon adjoining research facilities, vertical massing shall not exceed the physical cornice elevation of the Engineering Quad, benchmarked at an absolute ceiling of 24.0 meters above natural ground level.

Given the current project gateway, architectural submissions do not require interior partitions, fenestration mullions, or MEP distribution pathways. Deliverables are required at LOD 100 (conceptual volume, primary massing morphology, and orientation analysis).

3.0 Programmatic Allocation & Target Areas

The facility is intended to foster cross-disciplinary synergy. The initial brief outlined an aggregate target Gross Floor Area (GFA) of 6,500 sqm.

Spatial zoning requires that academic laboratories and core research spaces occupy forty percent (40%) of built volume. Incubators and commercial collaboration hubs must constitute exactly three-tenths (30%) of the total allocation. The balance of floor space is to be distributed between public amenities/circulation and accessible green roof terraces adhering to a strict 2:1 proportional ratio.

Project Addendum 01

Issued: October 24, 2026 | Priority: Critical Update
Notice to Tenderers: Information contained herein supersedes conflicting figures provided in previous briefs. Update all planning frameworks accordingly.

Item A: Subsurface Utility Realignment (GFA Revision)

Subsequent Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR) surveys revealed an unmapped municipal stormwater spine across Sector B's northern perimeter. A 6-meter protection setback is now legally enforced.

Consequently, the development envelope is compressed: the maximum permissible Gross Floor Area (GFA) previously listed as 6,500 sqm is officially amended and capped at 6,000 sqm.

Item B: Pedestrian Microclimate Standard

Due to the introduction of an open-air ground terrace and community cafe in the southwest court, wind turbulence thresholds must be tightened. Microclimate predictive simulations must satisfy comfort levels designated for Sitting rather than standard strolling or standing.

Key Terminology

CDE (Common Data Environment): Cloud repository coordinating all digital information throughout project lifecycles (Autodesk Construction Cloud).

LOD (Level of Development): Industry framework indicating the degree to which an element's geometry and associated information can be relied upon.

EUI (Energy Use Intensity): Metric denoting annual operational energy consumption relative to total floor area. The KLUST High-Performance Campus Directive sets a target ceiling of 90 kWh/m²/year for early-stage design modeling.

Forma Noise Standard: Acoustic environmental criteria utilized for predictive exterior modeling. Academic study envelopes must not experience ambient road/campus noise exceeding 55 dB(A) at the building envelope.
